What this proves — and what it does not

  • Complete Project Drive opening geometry, exported from the actual project DLL and scene loader. The loaded snapshot follows 120 simulation intervals at 240 Hz, including non-rigid tyre geometry.
  • CPU BVH intersections provide primary visibility. The unchanged production Vulkan shaders compute SDF lighting. Their existing exact near-surface triangle refinement remains enabled.
  • Diffuse-only material override retains authored base colours. Car-paint specular, transparent glazing and reflections are deliberately excluded from this comparison.
  • Rest and loaded are independent snapshots. This is not an interactive Frontier-window capture, continuous GPU deformation verification, or a hardware performance measurement.
