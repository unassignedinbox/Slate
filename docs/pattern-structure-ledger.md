# Pattern construction ledger — v7.6

## Counting rule

**200 entries: 174 ornamental compositions, 16 weave constructions and 10 stitch constructions.** This is not a claim of 200 carpet layouts or 200 unrelated rendering algorithms. Shared drawing vocabulary is intentional. Whole composition plans and interior constructions differentiate designs; colorways, border styles alone, random seeds, repeat density, global rotation and scale do not. Thirty-eight basic/legacy starters plus Blank are available separately and excluded. Ombre has one basic card and is not used to fill the advanced quota. Compatibility aliases have no cards.

This ledger makes the count inspectable, not a subjective quality certificate. Geometry hashes guard against exact duplicates but do not prove reference fidelity. None of the designs is represented as an authenticated historical reconstruction.
