Breach the Wall

You are the only thing still running on this beach — a beaten-up compact saloon with sandbags wired to the roof. The garrison wall is 480 metres inland. Between you and the breach: minefields, hedgehogs, dragon's teeth, wire, trenches and bunkers full of sentries who shoot anything with wheels. Behind you the tide is coming in, and it does not stop.

Get to the breach
Drive north to the glowing gate in the sea wall.
Use cover
Wrecked tanks, earth mounds and sandbags break the sentries' line of sight.
Roads are fast, roads are exposed
The gravel tracks are mine-free — and in full view of the wall.
Beat the tide
Low ground floods first. Drown and the mission is over.
