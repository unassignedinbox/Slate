How it breaks
1 · Hertzian cone crack nucleates under the contact.
2 · Cracks open normal to max principal tension → radial stars near the hit, concentric rings further out.
3 · Each m² of new crack costs 2·Gc joules, so cracks arrest on their own: dust at the impact, intact slabs at the edge.
4 · Anisotropy = character: grain (wood), bedding (rock), residual stress (tempered glass dices itself), high Gc (plastic tears).
