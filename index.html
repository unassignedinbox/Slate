DESIGN NOTE / 2026.09

AAA terrain requirements

The graph is built around the constraints that make an SDF erosion tool useful beyond a heightmap demo.

01
Keep topology volumetric

Store signed distance, wetness, deposited volume, and solid fraction in a bounded 3D field. Caves and undercuts must not collapse into a single top surface.

02
Separate alteration from transport

Particles detach material on contact, carry a typed cargo load, then deposit or settle when capacity and velocity drop. A settled particle cannot erode forever.

03
Make water a guided material system

River agents and the surface shader share a flow path and current vector, while surface visibility stays independent from transport.

04
Design for GPU scale

Ping-pong field passes, no per-step readback, stable particle metadata, deterministic seeds, and an audit path are the production direction for larger tiles.

Based on flexible particle/SDF erosion research and the supplied Frontier reference. Full notes: docs/terrain-requirements.md
