01

Not octahedral mapping

Octahedral mapping packs a direction onto a square. This demonstration does not unwrap each object. Every disc is a persistent position, normal, radius, material response, and irradiance record in world space.

02

Why the floor is covered

Triangle-uniform sampling starves a two-triangle plane while oversampling a finely tessellated ball. Here, ball candidates are proportional to triangle area and the floor owns a jittered lattice, so every receiving surface has predictable coverage.

03

Hybrid, not screen-only

Screen-space injection is useful for moving detail, but it loses off-camera emitters and changes with the camera. Keep a world-space static field, then add screen-visible records for dynamic geometry. Bake placement if desired; continue integrating light at runtime.
