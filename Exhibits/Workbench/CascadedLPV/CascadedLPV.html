01

Rasterize samples

A 256² reflective shadow map captures current positions, normals, materials, and sun-facing flux. Every second texel becomes a transient surfel; current G-buffer samples add visible movers and blockers, so stale geometry is replaced immediately.

02

Propagate off screen

Three snapped 32³ lattices cover 24, 64, and 160 metres around the camera. Directional RGB light is injected and propagated while camera and light-view surfaces form a small blocker field.

03

Restore local detail

The volume result is intentionally broad. A half-resolution, temporally validated screen pass adds short-range colour transfer and GTAO without pretending that screen-visible information is global.
