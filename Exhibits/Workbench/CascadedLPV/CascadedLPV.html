01

Rasterize samples

A 512² GI-only reflective shadow map yields up to 65,536 blue-noise 2×2 candidates. Fixed local-space mesh surfels separately follow the moving cyan emitter even when it leaves the RSM or camera.

02

Propagate off screen

Three persistent 48³ lattices cover 24, 64, and 160 metres. The near field retains two source reservoirs and six-face RGB; an independent packed 80³ blocker lattice samples three points across every near propagation link.

03

Restore local detail

Stabilized 2048²/1024²/1024² shadow cascades use 3×3 or 5×5 PCF. GPU timestamps adapt presentation scale, spatial RSM candidates, propagation, then PCF without ever resizing an LPV.
