01

Deferred light and shadows

A three-target material G-buffer feeds GGX direct lighting. The sun uses three stabilized atlas tiles with receiver-slope bias and selectable PCF; a moving local light has a six-face radial-depth shadow.

02

Faithful probe cache

The C++ baker captures static-scene irradiance into 720 L1 directional probes. Invalid samples are rejected and flood-filled, while six directional reach values suppress interpolation through walls. Moving objects receive—but never rewrite—the bake.

03

Screen trace and denoise

Half-resolution cosine rays march the camera depth representation. Misses fall back to volume probes. Spatial reuse, world-position temporal reprojection, neighbourhood clamping, and bilateral weights stabilize one or two rays per pixel.

04

Specular probe fallback

Low-roughness reflections first search the screen and then use a mip-filtered sphere probe. The probe can be recaptured explicitly; it is intentionally separate from diffuse volume probes, matching EEVEE’s split indirect-light architecture.

Scope and fidelity

This is an architectural WebGL2 reconstruction, not Blender source compiled for the browser. WebGL2 cannot expose EEVEE’s compute-driven sparse virtual-page allocator, so the exhibit preserves its important behaviour with a fixed clip-atlas analogue. It does preserve the baked/dynamic boundary: volume probes are static, direct lights and shadows are live, screen traces see only camera-visible information, and probe fallback fills misses.
