SPINE RUNNER

NEO-KOWLOON SPINE  ·  2.27 KM  ·  3 LAPS  ·  ANTI-GRAVITY CLASS

An elevated quarantine circuit through the Kowloon stacks. The deck is never yours alone: the city's infrastructure runs through the racing line. Twenty six hundred tonnes of maglev, municipal traffic, a wave of rising bollards and the yard cranes. Learn the rhythm, or learn the wall.

Dynamic track elements

◤ BOLLARD ZIPPERZipper Avenue · 56 m deck · ~1,150 bollards
over a thousand bollards rise in a travelling diagonal wave: two corridors stay open, the gap sliding across the deck row by row. A timed slalom at 300 km/h.

PULSE CALL (Z) — your teammate dives into the wave and draws the pattern toward your lane: the teeth collapse in front of you and a wide gap opens where you actually are. Cost: their capacitor. Watch it recharge.
◤ MAGLEV CROSSINGSEast Span · 3 crossings
Heavy freight crosses the deck perpendicular at ~380 km/h. Lamps flash, horns sound, gates drop. Crossings fire on staggered random clocks — sometimes back to back, sometimes a double consist. Time the gap or eat a train.
◤ TRAFFIC DISTRICTSouth Span · 4 lanes
Municipal hover-traffic crawls the deck: haulers, taxis, buses. Two of the four lanes run oncoming — headlights in your racing line. Thread it.
◤ MAGNETIC CRANESCrane Yard · Y-fork at 1.36 km, T-split at 1.53 km
Yard cranes swing capture cones across the deck. Enter a cone and the magnet lifts your ship off the deck, carries you, and throws you down the branch it has locked. Markers on the deck show which branch is claimed.

Y-FORK: two lanes, either side. T-SPLIT: the high-line climbs, the low-line dives. Fight the magnet with airbrakes to break lock — or ride it and take the boost on release.
